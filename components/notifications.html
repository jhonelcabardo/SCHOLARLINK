Notification Guidelines

  • ✔ Notify applicants immediately when requirements are incomplete.
  • ✔ Inform scholars about grade and COR submission deadlines.
  • ✔ Send approval or rejection notices after application review.
  • ✔ Remind scholars about orientation schedules and important events.
  • ✔ Keep messages clear, professional, and concise.
  • ✔ Use templates to save time and ensure consistency.
