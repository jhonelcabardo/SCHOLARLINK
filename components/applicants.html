Applicant Review Guide

  • ✔ Review the applicant's submitted application before making a decision.
  • ✔ Verify the applicant information and scholarship requirements.
  • ✔ Use Under Review while checking the application.
  • ✔ Use Request Revision if information or requirements need correction.
  • ✔ Use Qualified for Exam when the applicant passes the initial screening.
  • ✔ Schedule the scholarship examination after the applicant is qualified.
  • ✔ Applicants who pass the examination can proceed to Scholar Confirmation.
  • ✔ Once the applicant is confirmed as a scholar, the system creates the scholar record automatically.
