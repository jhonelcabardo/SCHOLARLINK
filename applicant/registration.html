2. Eligibility

SCHOLARLINK is intended for individuals who meet the eligibility requirements established by the applicable scholarship program administered by the City Government of Naga or its authorized scholarship office.

Creating an account does not automatically establish eligibility for a scholarship.

Applicants must satisfy the requirements, qualifications, documentary requirements, and other conditions prescribed by the applicable scholarship program.

3. Account Registration

Applicants are required to provide accurate and complete information when creating a SCHOLARLINK account.

Applicants certify that all information submitted through SCHOLARLINK is true, accurate, complete, and current to the best of their knowledge.

5. Submission of Documents

Applicants may be required to submit supporting documents through SCHOLARLINK.

Applicants are responsible for ensuring that all submitted documents are authentic, valid, readable, complete, and meet the requirements specified by the Scholarship Office.

Applicants must not upload falsified, altered, fraudulent, or unauthorized documents.

Submission of documents does not guarantee scholarship approval.

All scholarship applications are subject to review and verification by authorized personnel.

Application statuses may include Pending, Under Review, For Verification, Qualified, Qualified for Examination, Approved, Rejected, Disqualified, or other statuses determined by the Scholarship Office.
